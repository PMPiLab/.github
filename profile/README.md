# PMPiLab (PolyU Medical Physics Intelligence Lab)

PMPiLab is a research group at the Department of Health Technology and Informatics, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU), focusing on medical physics and intelligent healthcare technologies.


## Research

Our research interests include:

- **Magnetic Resonance Imaging:** MRF, MRE, reconstruction, and advanced MRI
- **Medical Image Computing:** segmentation, registration, synthesis, and multimodal analysis
- **Radiotherapy:** automated treatment planning, dose modeling, and adaptive radiotherapy
- **Medical AI:** intelligent methods for MRI, CT, digital pathology, and multimodal medical data


## Open Research

This organization hosts research code, reproducible implementations, tools, and resources developed by PMPiLab.

We welcome academic collaboration and contributions from the community.
